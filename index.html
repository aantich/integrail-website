What’s behind the numbers? +

These are scripted teaching examples, not live queries or measured comparisons with Codex or Claude. The first models 60,000 versus 600 LLM tokens and 60 versus 0.6 seconds; the second models 120,000 versus 600 tokens and 120 versus 0.6 seconds. Playback is compressed to 12 seconds.

The 100× query-cost illustration assumes the same price per token and excludes ingestion, graph extraction, embeddings, storage and infrastructure. Real results depend on the corpus, retrieval, model, caching and question. Agents can also use search indexes and retrieval tools; exhaustive document reading is one baseline workflow, not their only mode. Graph retrieval requires the relevant facts to have been correctly extracted and linked.

There’s research behind the approach.

Microsoft’s GraphRAG research found more comprehensive and diverse answers to global questions than a conventional RAG baseline. A separate systematic study found that combining retrieval approaches can improve performance.

That’s why Integrail pairs vector context with graph relationships. These studies support the design direction; they do not benchmark Integrail or establish a universal accuracy gain.
