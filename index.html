What’s behind the numbers? +

These are scripted teaching examples, not live queries or measured comparisons with Codex or Claude. The first models 60,000 versus 600 LLM tokens and 60 versus 0.6 seconds; the second models 120,000 versus 600 tokens and 120 versus 0.6 seconds. Playback is compressed to 12 seconds.

The 100× query-cost illustration assumes the same price per token and excludes ingestion, graph extraction, embeddings, storage and infrastructure. Real results depend on the corpus, retrieval, model, caching and question. Agents can also use search indexes and retrieval tools; exhaustive document reading is one baseline workflow, not their only mode. Graph retrieval requires the relevant facts to have been correctly extracted and linked.

Clear answers.

What is RAG as a service?+

Retrieval-augmented generation gives an AI model relevant company information at query time. Integrail provides the connected knowledge layer: ingestion, vectorized passages and graph facts, plus retrieval for your agents. Your provider hosts and operates the service.

Why combine graph and vector search?+

Vector search finds text with related meaning. Graph search follows explicit connections between entities, such as who invested in a company and who works there. Together they can supply both the source passage and the relationships a question depends on.

Does this replace Codex or Claude?+

Integrail gives those agents shared business context. You choose the agent; Integrail helps it retrieve connected knowledge instead of reconstructing the same facts from files with each question. Available runtimes and integrations are agreed for your deployment.

Where does our data live?+

Your documents, graph and vector index live in the infrastructure chosen with your hosting or managed service provider. Model inference can have a separate data boundary: using an external API sends the retrieved context to that model provider. Define location, access, retention and model routing as part of your deployment.

How do we get started?+

Start with one knowledge source, one useful workflow and a provider you trust. Define a pilot, evaluate answer quality and permissions on your own data, and agree the rollout with your provider.
