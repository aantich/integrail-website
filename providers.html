PARTNER QUESTIONS

Let’s get
specific.

Who is the partner offering for?+

Cloud providers, hosting businesses and managed service providers that want to deliver AI services from infrastructure they operate or manage. Start with customers who have a clear knowledge-intensive workflow and a need for control over where their data lives.

How do we make money?+

Potential service lines include onboarding and integration fees, recurring knowledge-hosting plans, retrieval usage, managed agents and custom workflow services. You design the customer package. Licensing, support responsibilities and partner economics are agreed with Integrail before launch.

Can we use our own cloud?+

That is the deployment model: the knowledge stack lives in your chosen environment. Infrastructure requirements, topology, isolation, backup and operational responsibilities are defined during pilot planning. Agent inference may use separately configured external model APIs.

Is this a white-label offering?+

The partner model is designed for you to sell and manage the customer service. Branding, licensing, billing integration and support arrangements are topics to agree in your partner plan rather than assumed contract terms.

What does the first pilot include?+

Define one customer, one knowledge source and one workflow, then agree success criteria for answer quality, permissions, freshness and query economics. The pilot establishes deployment fit and a commercial basis for a wider rollout.
